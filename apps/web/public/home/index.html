NZOSA

Free accounting for New Zealand.

Bank files in; a coded double-entry ledger, GST returns, rental schedules, IR3 and IR10 figures and financial reports out. It codes from your own past decisions, and every figure can be checked.

NZOSA is free. Help keep it that way.

There is no subscription and no paid tier. It is built and kept up to date with each year's IRD rates by one person, and donations are what keep it going.
