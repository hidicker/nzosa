Coding reconciliation

Load the coding from your previous system to compare it with NZOSA's. Where they agree, the coding can become a rule; where they differ, choose which is right. Lines are matched on amount and nearest date.

How to get the file out of Xero
  1. Go to Reporting → Account Transactions.
  2. Select all accounts.
  3. Under columns, select all columns.
  4. Set Grouping/Summarising to None. This matters: grouped output hides the account each posting went to, which is the whole point of the check.
  5. Click Update, then Export → Excel.

Load the resulting .xlsx or .csv here. A chart of accounts export helps too, so a code and a name are recognised as the same account.

Loading a spreadsheet instead

Your own spreadsheet is accepted, and needs three columns:

  • Date and Amount — required, and found by those headings.
  • A column saying what each line was coded to. Its heading can be What, Account, Code, Coding, Category or Class — whichever yours uses.

If none of those headings fits, the file is not refused: you are shown the headings it found and can point at the right three columns yourself. Nothing needs renaming first.

Invoices

Invoice payments are matched by invoice number, then recorded allocations, then exact amount and date. Anything less certain is suggested for you to confirm.

Bills

What is owed to suppliers. A bill is a draft until approved; approving it books the cost and its GST on the bill date. Match its payment on Reconcile.

Rental year end

Each rental property's year: its schedule, the property manager's statements, and trips made to it in your own vehicle. The owners' returns on Personal year end take their share of what is here.

Personal year end

A person's income tax return (IR3) for a year: what it still needs that these books cannot know, and the return itself, set out as a year-end pack to print or save as a PDF.

Rental information

Each tenancy's rent, rent changes and bond, compared with the rent received, showing whether the tenant is behind or ahead and by how much. This page does not change the books.

IRD records

What Inland Revenue holds, checked against these books: GST returns, any tax account’s transactions, the employer summary, and an IR3’s confirmation. Figures that differ are flagged; figures these books do not hold are listed so they can be entered.

Paste an IR3 confirmation

Open the filed IR3’s confirmation in myIR, select all its text (Ctrl+A), copy it and paste it here. It is read on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

GST reconciliation

Where to get the files

    What happens to duplicates

    Nothing is ever deleted automatically. Rows identical to one already in the ledger are marked duplicate and left out, so re-importing an overlapping statement is safe. Rows that only look like duplicates — the same amount and payee with a different bank serial, or a day apart on a feed with no serial — are marked review and kept, for you to decide.
